A little about me
“Dance is more than movement — it is a way to connect, express and inspire.”

A performer with a love for people and movement.

I am a South African dancer, instructor and performer with experience across cruise ship entertainment, dance education and live productions. My work brings together performance, guest engagement and the joy of helping people discover confidence through movement.

From leading social dance sessions at sea to building a dance community through Feel The Move, I value professionalism, adaptability and creating welcoming experiences for people of different backgrounds and abilities.
